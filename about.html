About BPMfinder.ca

BPMfinder.ca is a free browser tempo tool and an original guide library for people who make, play, or move to music.

Guide · Justin Helmer · Updated 2026-10-20 · 3 min read · about

Open free BPM tool All guides

What this site is

BPMfinder.ca provides a free tempo detector that runs in your browser and a set of practical guides about BPM, tap tempo, half-time, delay times, DJ workflow, and running cadence. The tool does not require an account, and audio is analyzed on your device rather than uploaded for tempo detection.

The site is published by Justin Helmer under the Excavationpro / LYGO workshop. It sits alongside excavationpro.ca (music and essays), asiancoastline.com (the free listening portal), deepseekoracle.com (AI and creator guides), eternalhaven.ca (books and games), and chatagent.ca (AI agent guides and tools).

Why it exists

Most BPM pages are thin tool shells. You drop a file, get a number, and learn nothing about why the number appeared or when to ignore it. The goal here is the opposite: a useful tool plus enough explanation that you can check the result, understand half-time and double-time, and use the tempo in a real session.

The tool was built for the cases where a full DAW is inconvenient: a phone, a laptop without plugins, a quick check before a DJ set, or a private demo that should not be uploaded to a stranger’s server.

How the guides are tested

Claims that can be measured are measured. The accuracy page reports a real test of the detector against generated click tracks, including the cases where it returned a half or double count or failed. Genre tempo ranges are presented as conventions, not rules. When a guide gives a number, it shows the arithmetic or the method behind it.

Editorial standards

  • Original writing, not scraped or auto-spun text.
  • One clear job per page; no doorway pages or keyword stuffing.
  • Visible byline and update date on every guide.
  • Corrections are made in place and noted in the update date.
  • Advertising is labelled and kept off tool, policy, and error pages.

How the tool is maintained

The tool is a static browser page, not a cloud service. There is no account database to breach and no upload queue to maintain. When the detector changes, the accuracy page is re-run and the date is updated. When a guide is corrected, the change is made in place and the update date moves.

If you find a repeatable bug, send the file format, the browser, the expected BPM, and the returned BPM. A reproducible report is worth more than a star rating.

The site is deliberately small: a tool, a guide library, and the policy pages needed to run it honestly. There is no account system, no newsletter pop-up, and no paywall. If that changes, this page will say so.

Advertising and support
