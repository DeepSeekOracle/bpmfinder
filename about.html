About BPMfinder.ca

A free educational BPM site and private browser tempo tool operated by Justin Helmer.

What we offer

BPMfinder.ca provides original guides about tempo detection and a free tool that analyzes audio in your browser. The goal is practical help for music makers — not clickbait, not malware, not a parked domain.
